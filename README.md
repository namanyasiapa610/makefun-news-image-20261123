Forty stories from eight regions, every one from that single day: thirteen numbered main cards with certainty chips and sources, a THE DAY IN NUMBERS box, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a UTC timeline, and a red DATE CHECK box naming the stories that were *not* from 23 November. The lead story is gold's slide to $3,538 an ounce, down from the 2026 record of $5,595. Pale sepia paper, midnight-blue ink, silver accents; all scenes are original drawings.
